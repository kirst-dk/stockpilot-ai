The Engine

Research. Allocate. Record on-chain.

StockPilot turns market signals into a transparent, auditable portfolio. The agent doesn't just trade — it leaves a permanent track record on Mantle.

Growth 60% Yield 25% Protection 15%
  • 01Gather signals. Nansen smart-money flows, ELFA sentiment and realized volatility.
  • 02Allocate. AI classifies the regime and targets a growth / protection / yield split.
  • 03Execute. Swaps route through Fluxion; assets bridge in via Relay.
  • 04Record. Every decision is written on-chain via recordDecision — verifiable on Mantlescan.
